QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How do I know if my shower is leaking behind the tile?

Soft baseboards, a musty smell, cracked or dark grout lines, and hollow-sounding tile are the common signs. We diagnose honestly. Sometimes it is a $400 regrout, sometimes the pan has failed.

Is grout waterproof?

No, grout and tile both pass moisture. The membrane behind them does the waterproofing, which is why we photograph and flood-test it before tile ever goes up.

Can you fix just the shower pan?

Sometimes. If the wall membrane is sound we can rebuild the pan alone. If the system behind the walls has failed, we will show you the evidence and price the honest fix.
