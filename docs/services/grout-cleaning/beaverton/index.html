QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does grout cleaning and sealing cost?

Most jobs land around $950, with a typical range of $750–$1,200 depending on the square footage and how many rooms we treat. Machine deep-cleaning, stain treatment, and penetrating sealer are all included — still the cheapest way to make a tiled room look new.

Will cleaning fix discolored grout?

Usually dramatically. Where stains are permanent, we can apply a color-seal that restores a uniform tone — or quote a regrout if the grout is failing rather than just dirty.

How often should grout be sealed?

High-traffic floors every 1–2 years; showers roughly every year. Sealed grout wipes clean instead of absorbing.
