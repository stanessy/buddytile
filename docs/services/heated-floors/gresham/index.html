QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does a heated tile floor cost to add?

Adding electric radiant heat to a bathroom floor we are already tiling typically runs about $950–$1,400 including the thermostat, depending on room size.

How much does a heated floor cost to run?

A typical bathroom mat draws about as much as a hair dryer while warming up, and a programmable thermostat only runs it when you use the room — most homeowners see a few dollars a month.

Can you add heat under my existing tile?

The mat has to go under the tile, so heat gets added when a floor is being redone — which is why we always ask about it before a floor project starts.
