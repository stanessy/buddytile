TILE FLOOR INSTALLATION IN PORTLAND, OR

Floors take the most abuse of any tile in the house: carts, dogs, dropped pans, wet boots. We prep the substrate like it matters, because it does: self-leveler where the floor dips, uncoupling membrane where it moves, and layout planned so the cuts land where you'll never see them.

QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does tile floor installation cost?

Labor and prep run about $16 per square foot on most floors with a $900 minimum, a 60 sq ft entry lands near $1,000, a 120 sq ft kitchen near $2,000, before tile selection.

Can you tile over my existing floor?

Sometimes over sound concrete or existing tile; never over floating floors. We check the substrate at the estimate and put the honest prep in writing.

What tile is best for entryways and mudrooms?

Porcelain with a textured finish. It shrugs off water, grit, and dog claws. We will bring samples rated for exactly that abuse.
