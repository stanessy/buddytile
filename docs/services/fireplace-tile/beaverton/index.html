QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does a tile fireplace surround cost?

Classic surrounds start around $1,200; floor-to-ceiling feature walls typically run $2,500-$4,500 in labor depending on height and material, before tile selection.

Can you tile over a brick fireplace?

Usually yes, sound brick takes a scratch coat or backer and then tile or stone directly, which is far cleaner than demolition.

Is it safe to tile around a wood stove or firebox?

With heat-rated setting materials and correct clearances, yes. We follow the manufacturer clearances for your specific insert or stove.
