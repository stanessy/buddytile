QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does shower regrouting cost?

Most full shower regrouts run $550-$900 including re-caulking. Compare that to a $6,000+ remodel, when the tile and waterproofing are sound, regrouting is the smart money.

How do I know if I need regrouting or a new shower?

Cracked or powdery grout with solid, well-bonded tile usually means regrout. Hollow-sounding tile, soft walls, or recurring mold point deeper. We check honestly before quoting either way.

How long does regrouting take?

Most showers take one to two days, and you can usually shower again 24 hours after we seal.
