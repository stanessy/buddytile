FAQ

Questions we hear every week

How much does a custom tile shower cost in Vancouver WA?

Most full tile showers run $7,000-$10,500 for labor and waterproofing, depending on size and features like niches, benches, or curbless entries. Finish tile is priced with your selections, and your written in-home estimate is free.

How long does a custom tile shower take?

Most showers run 5-8 working days from demo to grout: tear-out, waterproofing with a flood test, tile, then grout and seal. If you need it faster and like a seamless look, ask about our one-day shower systems.

What waterproofing do you use?

RedGard. Every shower gets a full liquid membrane over cement board, a flood-tested pan, and photos of the waterproofing before tile covers it.

Can you convert my tub to a custom tile shower?

Yes. We remove the tub, rebuild the wet area with proper waterproofing, and tile a walk-in or curbless shower, usually within a week or two.

How much does a full bathroom remodel cost?

Full remodels are priced from a written scope, because tile, vanity, fixtures, and any plumbing or wall changes drive the number. Your free in-home design visit produces that scope and price the same day, and it does not change unless you change the plan.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most hall and primary bath remodels run two to four weeks from demo to final walkthrough. You get a written schedule before we start and daily photos while we work.

How much does a heated tile floor cost to add?

Adding electric radiant heat to a bathroom floor we are already tiling typically runs about $950-$1,400 including the thermostat, depending on room size.
