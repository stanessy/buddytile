HOW MUCH DOES A TILE SHOWER COST IN VANCOUVER WA? (2026 PRICES)

August 21, 2026 · Buddy Tile

Every week someone asks us what a tile shower actually costs, and every week the internet gives them a range so wide it is useless. Here are our real numbers.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Most full custom tile showers we build in Vancouver and Portland run $6,200 to $10,500 for labor and waterproofing. Tub-to-shower conversions start around $5,600. Finish tile is priced with your selections on top.

WHAT MOVES THE PRICE

  • Size, a 3x3 stall and a 5-foot walk-in are different animals
  • Niches, benches, and corner shelves, each adds labor
  • Curbless entry, the recessed pan and linear drain add work below the tile
  • Tile choice, large format and mosaic both price higher than standard field tile
  • Glass, frameless doors run more than sliders

WHEN YOU SHOULD NOT REMODEL

If your tile is sound and the grout is the problem, a regrout runs $550 to $900 and makes the shower read brand new. We will tell you honestly which one you need at the free visit.

WHAT IS ALWAYS INCLUDED WITH US

  • Full Schluter or equivalent waterproofing, flood-tested before tile
  • Photos of the waterproofing before it disappears behind tile
  • A written same-day estimate, no card fees, one warranty
